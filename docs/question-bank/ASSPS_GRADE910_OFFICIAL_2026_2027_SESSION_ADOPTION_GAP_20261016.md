## Examination-year applicability safeguard

PECTAA's [7 November 2025 revised Grade IX ALP notification](https://pectaa.edu.pk/uploads/Revised%20ALP%20Notification%2007.11.2025.pdf) explicitly concerns the **Annual Examination 2026**. It must **not** be auto-applied to a putative **2027 examination**, or to every Grade IX–X school assessment in session 2026–27. A catalog listing marked 2026–27 alone also does not prove an ASSPS adopted book, medium, subject stream, printed exercise page or correct-answer key.
